FAVICON / APP ICONS
  favicon.svg                 512x512, bare mark, adapts automatically:
                              dark ink on a light tab strip, cream on a dark one.

  app-icon.svg                512x512, olive tile with rounded corners (for PayPal,

  app-icon-192/512.png        PWA / Android sizes.

COLOURS USED
  ink        #212E24   wordmark
  tagline    #2F3F37
  olive      #454B37   tile background
  vellum     #F4F1E9   cream / reversed artwork
  sage       #C9CCBF   reversed tagline

NOTE
  The wordmark and tagline are traced outlines, not live text. They cannot be
  re-typed or edited as type, and they do not depend on any font being installed.
