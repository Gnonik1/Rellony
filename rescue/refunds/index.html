Refund policy

Rellony Rescue · last updated 15 October 2026

You only pay when we can help. The free check looks at your rating, your first answers and anything you tell us about your council’s letter before you pay. If we can’t help — a rating of 0, a formal notice or closure, a mention of illness or an allergic reaction, or a business that isn’t a caterer — we tell you who can, and you’re never asked to pay.

After you’ve paid, we refund the full £79 if:

  • it comes to light after payment that we can’t help (for example, you tell us later that your letter shows a formal notice);
  • your pack isn’t released within 24 hours of you finishing the questions and you ask us to cancel;
  • the FSA’s pack changes or is withdrawn before your pack is released so that we can’t fill it in, and you ask us to cancel;
  • within 14 days of day 0 you show us an error we caused — for example the pack doesn’t match your answers or the letter points you ticked — and we can’t correct it within 2 working days;
  • you were charged twice for the same order.

We don’t refund:

  • because a council later gives the same or a lower rating, refuses a re-visit request, or charges a fee — those are council decisions, and we never promise an outcome;
  • a change of mind once your pack has been released — it is made for your business and delivered straight away.

How: email rescue@rellony.com from the address you paid with. Approved refunds go back through PayPal, normally within 2 working days of approval; your bank or PayPal may take longer to show it.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Rellony Rescue is a self-help service. We don’t visit premises, contact councils or promise ratings — the officer decides. Independent: not part of the FSA or any council.
