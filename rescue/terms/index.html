Rellony Rescue · Version 1.0 · last updated 15 October 2026

Plain-English summary (not a substitute for the terms below): Rellony Rescue is a self-help service for food businesses. Our software fills in the FSA’s own Safer food, better business pack from your answers on your device, gives you a 4-week plan, emails you for four weeks and gives you a self-check. We don’t visit you, check your work, contact your council or promise a rating. Food safety stays your responsibility. £79 in total; refunds as set out in our Refund policy.

1. Who we are

Rellony Rescue is provided by Giorgi Nonikashvili, an individual trading as Rellony, based in Tbilisi, Georgia (rescue@rellony.com) ("we", "us"). Our full postal address and other legal information are at rellony.com/legal.

2. What the service is

Rellony Rescue is a digital self-help service for food businesses in England. When you buy it, you get:

(a) your pack, in two parts: (i) your SFBB pack — the Food Standards Agency’s own Safer food, better business for caterers (the PDF the FSA publishes on GOV.UK, unaltered), whose form fields our software fills in from your answers, in your browser on your own device; and (ii) your Rescue plan, written by our software — a 4-week plan listing the gaps found in your answers (and in your council’s letter, if you tell us what it says), with actions taken from our library based on official guidance and the page of your SFBB pack each action relates to; a continuation of your cleaning schedule where it needs more rows; an allergen matrix template; a daily fridge and freezer log (optional, unless your council’s letter raises temperature records); and your council’s re-visit details where available — online and as a PDF;

(b) the programme — emails on days 3, 7, 14 and 21 after your pack is released, and a final email on day 28;

(c) the self-check — a 10-question checklist you answer yourself, a tool that makes a PDF from your own photos on your own device, and a draft re-visit request you can edit and send yourself;

(d) email support — answers, within 2 working days, to questions about using your pack, until day 35.

3. What the service is not

We do not: visit or inspect your premises or check the work you do; contact your council or act on your behalf; decide or verify allergen information; give legal advice or professional advice specific to your premises; certify, approve or assess your business — the self-check is your own checklist; speak for the Food Standards Agency or your council; or promise or predict any food hygiene rating, inspection outcome or council decision. Only your council’s officer decides your rating.

7. How the pack is made

Our software fills in your SFBB pack and builds your Rescue plan from your answers using fixed rules; no AI writes either. We tick a "Yes" in your SFBB pack only where your answers say you already do what it asks; everything else is left for you. If you tell us what your council’s letter says, each point you tick is added to your plan as you told us it. Your SFBB pack is the FSA’s material, used under the Open Government Licence v3.0: we fill in its form fields and change nothing else, and we are not responsible for its content. Parts of the Rescue plan adapt public sector information licensed under the same licence. The FSA has not endorsed Rellony, your filled-in SFBB pack or the Rescue plan. Scoring-table information describes how the FSA’s published table works, not a prediction.

We aim to release your pack within 24 hours of you finishing the questions — usually within minutes. Day 0 is the day your pack is released. The programme ends on day 28 and email support on day 35. Your SFBB pack is made from the version of the FSA’s pack our software was built for. If the FSA changes or withdraws it so that we can’t fill it in, we’ll tell you; you can still download the FSA’s blank pack from GOV.UK and follow your Rescue plan, and we’ll update the software as soon as we reasonably can. If this happens before your pack is released, you can cancel for a full refund.

Before you pay, you see a free check based on your rating, your first answers and anything you tell us about your council’s letter.
